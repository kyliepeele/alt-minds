What makes me stand out?

Warm, affirming, and collaborative care

I provide a space that is affirming, collaborative, and real. My work centers clients who want support exploring identity, relationships, sexuality, and healing without shame.

Therapy with me is warm, thoughtful, and grounded in helping you feel more connected to yourself, your body, and your relationships.

My practice is especially supportive of LGBTQIA+ clients, kink and BDSM communities, and ethical non-monogamy.

My personal experiences in mental health and the medical field also inform my approach to coping with mental health and chronic illness issues for my clients. Though I have experience in the kink and the ENM worlds, I think you are the expert in your life and relationships, so I encourage self autonomy while providing alternative views to insure your safety within your relationships.
