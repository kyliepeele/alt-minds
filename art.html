Accelerated Resolution Therapy

"Keep the knowledge, lose the pain"

What is A.R.T.?

Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART) is a brief, evidence-based form of psychotherapy that works by reprogramming how distressing memories and images are stored in the brain. Unlike traditional talk therapy, ART does not require you to share the details of your trauma out loud — the work happens internally, guided by the therapist.

ART combines elements of several established therapies, including eye movement techniques similar to EMDR, with a unique process called Voluntary Memory and Image Replacement (VIR), which allows you to replace painful imagery with something neutral or positive — keeping the facts of what happened, but releasing the emotional charge attached to them.

What is it used for?

  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Anxiety and panic
  • Depression
  • Phobias
  • Grief and loss
  • Sleep disturbances and nightmares
  • Performance anxiety
  • Chronic pain and somatic symptoms
  • OCD and intrusive thoughts

What does a session look like?

ART is typically completed in just 1 to 5 sessions, making it one of the fastest-working trauma therapies available. During a session, you will follow the therapist's hand with your eyes in a series of smooth, guided movements — similar to watching something pass back and forth. While doing so, you will hold an image or memory in mind and allow your brain to process and rewrite it at its own pace.

You remain in full control throughout the process. The therapist guides the structure, but you decide what to work on and how much to share. Many clients report feeling noticeably lighter or calmer within a single session, even when working on deeply painful experiences.
